Seed

A private reading and learning project.

Seed is a personal library and study tool, still being built. It runs on its owner's own server and is not open to the public.

Seed backup

Seed backup is the small part of Seed that keeps an off-site copy of the server's backups in its owner's Google Drive. It encrypts every backup before uploading it, and it can only see the files it has created itself.

Details are in the privacy policy.
