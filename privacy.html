Privacy policy

Seed backup · last updated 12 November 2026

What Seed backup is

Seed backup is a tool used by one person, the owner of the Seed server, to keep an off-site copy of that server's backups in the owner's own Google Drive. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it can access in Google Drive

It asks Google only for the drive.file permission. With it, Seed backup can create files in the owner's Drive and read, change or delete only the files it created. It cannot see, open or list any other file in that Drive.

What it stores

It stores encrypted backups of the Seed server: database copies and stored files. They are encrypted on the server before they are uploaded, including their names, with a key Google does not have, so Google cannot read them.

What it does not do

  • It does not read, collect or share any other Google account data.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone, and uses no advertising or analytics.
  • This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.

Keeping and deleting

Older backups are removed automatically on a schedule. Revoking Seed backup's access in the Google account's security settings stops it immediately; the files it created stay in Drive until deleted.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Seed backup's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
